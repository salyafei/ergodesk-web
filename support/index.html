Common questions

Which devices does ErgoDesk work on?
iPhones and iPads that support ARKit, and Android phones that support Google Play Services for AR (ARCore). AR does not run in simulators or most emulators. On devices without AR, the app shows a message instead of the camera view.
The desk never turns blue.
Improve the lighting, move the phone slowly from side to side, aim at a part of the desk with some visible texture and keep the phone about 30 to 60 cm away. Plain white, glossy or black surfaces are harder to detect.
The zones point the wrong way.
Use the rotate buttons to turn them, or tap Rescan and tap closer to the desk edge directly in front of you.
How do I switch between centimetres and inches?
Once the zones are placed, tap the cm / in button. The Aa button hides or shows the labels.
I denied camera access.
Open your device Settings, find ErgoDesk and allow Camera. The app uses the camera only on your device and never records or uploads anything. See the Privacy Policy.
Is ErgoDesk medical advice?
No. ErgoDesk is a visual planning aid based on general workstation guidance for an average adult. It is not a medical device. If you have pain or a health condition, speak to a qualified professional.
